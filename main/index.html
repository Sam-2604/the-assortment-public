The Assortment

a collection of moments

by Samarth Goradia

| |

A personal archive of feelings expressed through image and sound.
Each entry is a moment / representation - not named and not explained.
Just felt.
It's a digital shelf for the things that made me pause.
The things that made everything make sense.
🌏❤️🫂

The Assortment existed previously on my Instagram's Close Friends stories.
The Assortment began as a way to express without language as a barrier.
Language can diminish the raw feeling of an emotion.
Some things are just felt and never said.
📷🎵🎨🌊🌍🌅🌌

I expressed these moments and they hold their own meaning / interpretation for me.
Art is open to interpretation.
This moment could mean something entirely different to you, and if I tell you my interpretation, it ruins / steals yours.
Your take is for you to express what that moment means to you.
Safe Space 🫂❤️
